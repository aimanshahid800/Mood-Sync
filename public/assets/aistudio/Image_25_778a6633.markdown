## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, non-punitive productivity environment. It counters the sterile, anxiety-inducing pressure of traditional task managers through a soft, sensory-driven aesthetic rooted in emotional balance. The target audience encompasses individuals navigating neurodivergence, burnout, emotional fatigue, or simply seeking an intentional, gentle daily rhythm.

The design movement merges modern organic minimalism with soft tactile physical surfaces and frosted glass elements:
- Generous breathing room and plush whitespace create an unhurried, calm headspace.
- Card architectures suggest physical stationery and tactile tokens rather than rigid software containers.
- Color shifts are restorative and emotional rather than alarming—alert states use warm coral/candy tones rather than harsh crimson reds.
- The interface feels responsive, kind, and supportive, using soft transitions and deep, velvety ambient shadows.

## Colors

The palette balances soothing grounding neutrals with soft expressive hues that evoke emotional states and energetic levels without overstimulation.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#877499` Heather):** The guiding emotional anchor; applied to key focus states, navigational highlights, and primary actions.
- **Secondary (`#679F9F` Viridian):** Represents restored energy and balance; used for completed state validations, calm energy tags, and mindful check-in indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#E78299` Candy):** Provides warmth, delight, and gentle urgency; designated for active reflections, high-priority mood highlights, and pulse markers.
- **Accent (`#7D97C9` Azur):** A tranquil dusty blue applied to restorative, low-cognitive-load activities and reflective audio sessions.
- **Neutral Base (`#E6D4BF` Sandstone):** The warm, creamy backdrop replacing stark white canvases to eliminate glare and reduce cognitive strain.
- **High-Contrast Text (`#2D2633` Dark Charcoal):** A rich, deep plum-charcoal delivering AA/AAA contrast over Sandstone and soft white surfaces without the starkness of pure black.
- **Surface Tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` Soft Cloud):** Frosted glass container layer that floats effortlessly over Sandstone backdrops.

### Surface System
- **App Background:** `#F8F3ED` (tinted warm cream) to `#E6D4BF` (Sandstone gradient or section fills).
- **Default Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` or 100% solid `#FFFFFF` with warm-tinted ambient shadows.
- **Muted Container Surface:** Semi-transparent tints of the core emotional palette (e.g., Heather at 8% opacity for neutral input backgrounds).

## Typography

Quicksand’s geometry and rounded terminals project openness, approachability, and calm reassurance. Letter spacing is deliberately generous to support fast, stress-free legibility.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used exclusively for warm, greeting moments (e.g., "Good morning, Maya", "How is your energy right now?").
- **Medium & Small Headlines:** Anchor task schedule groupings, mood check-in modules, and audio prompt headers.
- **Body Styles:** Body-lg and Body-md maintain comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to avoid dense text walls, fostering a feeling of calm spaciousness.
- **Labels:** Set in Medium and Bold weights to ensure legibility across chip pills, progress tags, and timestamp counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a single-column, card-stacking model on mobile devices, transitioning to an asymmetric two-column sanctuary on tablets.

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Base Grid:** 4px baseline grid with an 8px preferred structural scale.
- **Mobile Margins:** Fixed 20px (`space-lg`) lateral margins to provide intentional edge padding, preventing content from crowding screen boundaries.
- **Inter-Card Spacing:** 16px (`space-md`) vertical separation between stacked schedule cards; 24px (`space-xl`) between distinct functional domains (e.g., Mood Slider vs. Task List).
- **Floating Clearance:** A dedicated bottom inset of `88px` (`bottom-bar-clearance`) on scrollable views ensures the floating pill navigation bar never covers interactive content.
- **Tablet / Expanded View:** Fluid centered content container locked to a maximum width of `640px` for focused, mindful single-tasking, avoiding sprawling desktop layouts that trigger cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive, harsh drop shadows in favor of multi-layered, tinted ambient diffusion. Shadows incorporate subtle traces of Heather (`#877499`) and Dark Charcoal (`#2D2633`) rather than sterile, cool black.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Soft Sandstone background canvas.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Chips):** 
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(135, 116, 153, 0.06);`
  - Evokes tactile card stock resting lightly on a wooden or fabric mat.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Modals):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(135, 116, 153, 0.08);`
  - Applied to picked-up tasks during drag-to-reorder, active reflection prompts, or expanded mood pickers.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bar & Raised Action Hub):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.12), 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(231, 130, 153, 0.12);`
  - Paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` on semi-transparent backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`).
- **Pressed / Sunk State:** Inset shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(45, 38, 51, 0.08)` providing physical feedback when mood chips and buttons are pressed.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy prioritizes organic curves and continuous rounded corners. Sharp edges are deliberately excluded from the system to evoke safety, friendliness, and emotional warmth.

### Token Application
- **Pill (Full Round / 9999px):** Standard interactive buttons, status tags, floating navigation bars, chip selectors, and audio playback scrubbers.
- **Container Level (20px – 24px / 1.25rem – 1.5rem):** All task cards, reflection modules, dialog sheets, and notification alerts.
- **Inner Nested Elements (12px – 16px):** Nested icons, energy meter segments, thumbnail illustrations, and embedded audio controls.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Pill Button:** Full rounded (`rounded-full`), height 52px. Filled with Heather (`#877499`) with white Quicksand label (Bold, 15px). Light upward highlight gradient (10% lighter top stop) paired with Level 1 warm shadow.
- **Secondary / Soft Button:** Full rounded pill, height 48px. Background in Viridian Tint (`rgba(103, 159, 159, 0.15)`), text colored in Dark Viridian (`#3D6B6B`).
- **Tactile Squeeze:** On touch/press, all buttons scale to `0.97` with a rapid 150ms spring transition.

### Floating Bottom Navigation
- **Pill Shell:** Floats 20px above the screen bottom; horizontally centered with 24px lateral inset. Height 68px.
- **Backdrop:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and Level 3 ambient shadow.
- **Magic Center Button:** Centered circular/pill element (56px × 56px) raised -14px above the bar line. Filled with Candy (`#E78299`) with an Azur-tinted glow shadow. Tapping opens the mood quick-log drawer.
- **Active Navigation Bubble:** Floating pill capsule that glides horizontally beneath the selected icon using a spring curve (`tension: 300, friction: 25`).

### Mood Check-In Chips
- **Geometry:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding 10px 18px.
- **Default State:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border in Sandstone (`#E6D4BF`), text in Dark Charcoal (`#2D2633`).
- **Selected State:** Shifts smoothly to solid state based on mood family (e.g., Heather for contemplative, Candy for high-energy, Viridian for grounded). Accompanied by a gentle scale pop (`1.05`) and Level 1 shadow.

### Energy-Aligned Task Schedule Cards
- **Structure:** 20px rounded card, white base, Level 1 shadow, 16px padding.
- **Energy Badge Indicator:** A vertical pill on the left border (width 4px, height 36px) or a pill tag displaying the energy cost: "Low Battery / Restorative" (Azur), "Balanced" (Viridian), or "Creative Spark" (Candy).
- **Completion Checkbox:** 24px circular toggle (not a box). When unchecked: 2px border in Sandstone. When checked: fills with Viridian, displays an organic checkmark, and applies a gentle strikethrough with 50% opacity fade to the task title.

### Gentle Energy Meters
- **Track:** 12px thick pill-shaped track filled with warm sand (`rgba(230, 212, 191, 0.45)`).
- **Indicator Fill:** Continuous fluid gradient transitioning from Azur (`#7D97C9`) through Viridian (`#679F9F`) to Candy (`#E78299`).
- **Thumb:** 24px white circle with an embedded centered pip matching the active zone color, floating with a soft Level 2 shadow.

### Mindful Reflection & Audio Prompts
- **Surface:** Subtle horizontal card with a muted gradient wash (Sandstone into soft Heather tint).
- **Audio Control:** 44px circular play/pause button with tactile inset when pressed. Paired with a soft wavy soundscape visualizer rendered in Heather and Viridian.
